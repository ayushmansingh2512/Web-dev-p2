Idea Generation Challenges and Method

Introduction

Ideation is a critical phase in Design Thinking where the goal is to generate a wide range of creative solutions to the defined problem. This section explores common challenges in idea generation and introduces the Visualize, Empathize, and Ideate (VEI) method as a structured approach to overcome these hurdles.

Key Aspects

  • Challenges in Idea Generation: Understand common obstacles such as fixation, fear of failure, and lack of diversity in ideas.
  • Visualize, Empathize, and Ideate (VEI) Method: Learn a systematic approach that combines visual thinking, empathetic understanding, and structured ideation techniques to foster innovation.
  • Overcoming Barriers: Discover strategies to break free from conventional thinking and encourage a more expansive and imaginative ideation process.

Expected Outcome

You will be equipped with a deeper understanding of ideation challenges and a practical method to generate more innovative and diverse solutions in your design projects.
