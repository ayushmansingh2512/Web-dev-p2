Activity: Project Opportunity and Scope

Introduction

This activity focuses on the crucial initial steps of any Design Thinking project: identifying a compelling opportunity and clearly defining its scope. A well-defined scope ensures that the project remains focused and achievable, while a clear understanding of the opportunity drives innovation.

Key Steps

  • Opportunity Identification: Explore various problem spaces and user needs to pinpoint a significant opportunity for design intervention.
  • Scope Definition: Clearly articulate the boundaries of the project, including what will and will not be covered, to manage expectations and resources effectively.
  • Stakeholder Alignment: Engage with relevant stakeholders to ensure a shared understanding and buy-in for the project's direction.

Expected Outcome

By the end of this activity, you will have a well-articulated project opportunity and a clearly defined scope, setting a strong foundation for the subsequent phases of the Design Thinking process.
