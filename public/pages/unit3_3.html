Ideation Tools: HMW and Storyboard

Introduction

In the Ideation phase of Design Thinking, specific tools help translate broad ideas into concrete concepts. This section focuses on two powerful ideation tools: "How Might We" (HMW) questions and Storyboarding, which facilitate creative problem-solving and visual communication of ideas.

Key Tools

  • "How Might We" (HMW) Questions: Learn to reframe challenges as opportunity statements, encouraging a wide range of innovative solutions. HMW questions are open-ended and foster creative thinking.
  • Storyboarding: Discover how to visually represent user experiences and proposed solutions in a sequential narrative. Storyboards help to visualize the user's interaction with a product or service over time.

Expected Outcome

You will gain practical skills in using HMW questions to spark ideation and storyboarding to develop and communicate your design concepts effectively, leading to more compelling solutions.
