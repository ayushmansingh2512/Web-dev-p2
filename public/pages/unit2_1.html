Empathy: Role, Phases, and Application

Introduction

Empathy is the cornerstone of Design Thinking, enabling a deep understanding of user needs, motivations, and behaviors. This section explores the pivotal role of empathy, its distinct phases, and practical applications within the design process.

Key Aspects

  • Role of Empathy: Discover how empathy drives human-centered innovation and problem-solving.
  • Phases of Empathy: Learn about the stages involved in cultivating and applying empathy, from observation to immersion.
  • Application in Design: Understand how empathetic insights translate into meaningful design solutions that resonate with users.

Expected Outcome

By the end of this section, you will grasp the fundamental importance of empathy in Design Thinking and be equipped to apply empathetic approaches to uncover genuine user needs.
