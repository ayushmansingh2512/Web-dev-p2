Activity: Apply Empathy and Define

Introduction

This activity provides a hands-on opportunity to apply the principles and tools of the Empathize and Define phases of Design Thinking. By working through a practical scenario, you will gain experience in understanding user needs and formulating a clear problem statement.

Key Steps

  • Empathize: Conduct user research, interviews, and observations to gather insights into user behaviors, needs, and pain points.
  • Synthesize: Organize and interpret the gathered data to identify patterns, themes, and key insights.
  • Define: Formulate a human-centered problem statement that clearly articulates the core challenge to be addressed.

Expected Outcome

You will successfully apply empathetic research methods and problem-framing techniques to arrive at a well-defined problem statement, ready for the ideation phase.
