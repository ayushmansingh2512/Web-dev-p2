Activity: Create Prototypes for Ideas

Introduction

This activity provides a hands-on opportunity to apply the methods of the Prototype Phase by creating tangible representations of your selected ideas. Prototyping allows for early testing and validation, transforming abstract concepts into concrete forms that can be evaluated and refined.

Key Steps

  • Idea Selection: Choose the most promising ideas from the Ideation Phase to develop into prototypes.
  • Prototype Creation: Build prototypes using appropriate materials and fidelity levels, focusing on key functionalities and user interactions.
  • Preparation for Testing: Prepare your prototypes for user testing, considering how to effectively gather feedback on their design and functionality.

Expected Outcome

You will successfully create prototypes for your innovative ideas, gaining valuable experience in translating concepts into testable forms and preparing for user feedback.
