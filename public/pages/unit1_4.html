Activity: Explore Possibilities and Brief

Introduction

This activity guides you through the process of exploring a wide range of possibilities for your design challenge and culminates in the preparation of a comprehensive design brief. A well-crafted brief serves as a foundational document, aligning the team's efforts and setting clear objectives for the design process.

Key Steps

  • Possibility Exploration: Brainstorm and research diverse approaches and solutions without immediate judgment, fostering creative thinking.
  • Information Synthesis: Consolidate gathered insights and potential solutions into a coherent overview.
  • Design Brief Preparation: Develop a detailed design brief that outlines the problem, objectives, target audience, constraints, and desired outcomes.

Expected Outcome

You will have a clear understanding of potential directions for your project and a finalized design brief that will steer your design efforts effectively.
