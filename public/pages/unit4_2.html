Prototyping Process: Fidelity and MVP

Introduction

This section delves into the strategic aspects of the prototyping process, emphasizing the importance of fidelity and the concept of a Minimum Viable Prototype (MVP). Understanding these elements is crucial for efficient and effective development of design solutions.

Key Concepts

  • Why We Prototype: Explore the core reasons for prototyping, including validating assumptions, gathering user feedback, and reducing development risks.
  • Fidelity for Prototypes: Learn about different levels of prototype fidelity (low, medium, high) and how to choose the appropriate level for various stages of the design process.
  • Minimum Viable Prototype (MVP) Process: Understand the MVP concept as a strategy to quickly build and test the core functionality of a solution with minimal resources.

Expected Outcome

You will gain a clear understanding of the prototyping process, enabling you to make informed decisions about prototype fidelity and effectively utilize the MVP approach to accelerate your design projects.
