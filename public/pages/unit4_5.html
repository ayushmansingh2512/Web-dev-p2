Activity: Test, Iterate, and Present Solution

Introduction

This culminating activity integrates the entire Design Thinking process, focusing on the critical steps of testing prototypes, iterating based on feedback, and effectively presenting your final solution. It emphasizes the importance of continuous refinement and compelling communication.

Key Steps

  • User Testing: Conduct thorough user testing of your prototypes to gather comprehensive feedback on usability, desirability, and effectiveness.
  • Iteration: Analyze user feedback and iterate on your design, making necessary adjustments and improvements to enhance the solution.
  • Solution Presentation: Craft a compelling narrative using the storytelling method to present your refined solution, highlighting its value and impact.

Expected Outcome

You will successfully test and iterate on your design, culminating in a well-refined solution that is effectively communicated through a compelling presentation, demonstrating your mastery of the Design Thinking process.
