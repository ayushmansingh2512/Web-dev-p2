Activity: Generate Innovative Solutions

Introduction

This activity provides a practical application of the Ideate Phase methods, challenging you to generate a diverse range of innovative solutions for a given design problem. It emphasizes creative exploration and the development of novel ideas.

Key Steps

  • Brainstorming: Utilize various brainstorming techniques to produce a large quantity of ideas without judgment.
  • Concept Development: Select promising ideas and develop them further into more detailed concepts.
  • Refinement: Iterate on the concepts, incorporating feedback and refining them for clarity and impact.

Expected Outcome

You will successfully apply ideation methods to generate a portfolio of innovative solution ideas, demonstrating your ability to think creatively and solve complex problems.
