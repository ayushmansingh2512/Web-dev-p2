Prototyping: Concept, Mindset, and Examples

Introduction

Prototyping is a fundamental aspect of Design Thinking, allowing ideas to be transformed into tangible forms for testing and iteration. This section explores the core concept of what a prototype is, the mindset required for effective prototyping, and various examples across different product types.

Key Aspects

  • Concept of a Prototype: Understand that a prototype is any tangible representation of an idea, from a sketch to a functional model, used to test assumptions and gather feedback.
  • Prototyping as a Mindset: Embrace prototyping as a way of thinking—a continuous cycle of making, testing, and learning, rather than a one-off activity.
  • Examples for Products: Explore diverse examples of prototypes, including physical models, digital mockups, role-playing scenarios, and service blueprints.

Expected Outcome

You will gain a comprehensive understanding of prototyping, enabling you to effectively create and utilize prototypes to validate ideas, reduce risks, and accelerate the design process.
